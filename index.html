Menstrual Tips
🌸

Simple tips for menstrual health and comfort.

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water throughout the day to support hydration and overall well-being.

Tap to view ›

Eat Nutritious Foods

Choose balanced meals that include vegetables, fruits, protein, and whole grains.

Tap to view ›

Get Enough Rest

Give your body enough time to rest and recover, especially when experiencing menstrual discomfort.

Tap to view ›

Keep Good Hygiene

Change menstrual products regularly and keep the body clean and comfortable.

Tap to view ›

Track Your Symptoms

Recording symptoms can help you understand patterns throughout your menstrual cycle.

Tap to view ›

Use Gentle Heat

A warm heating pad or warm compress may help ease menstrual cramps and provide comfort.

Tap to view ›

Stay Physically Active

Gentle movement such as walking, stretching, or light exercise can help you stay active during your period.

Tap to view ›

Manage Stress

Take time to relax through breathing exercises, quiet activities, or other healthy ways of managing stress.

Tap to view ›

Choose Comfortable Clothing

Wear comfortable, breathable clothing that helps you feel relaxed during your menstrual days.

Tap to view ›

Prepare Your Period Essentials

Keep menstrual products and other personal essentials available so you are prepared when your period starts.

Tap to view ›

Get Enough Iron-Rich Foods

Include iron-containing foods such as leafy greens, beans, eggs, meat, or fortified foods as part of balanced meals.

Tap to view ›

Limit Too Much Caffeine

If caffeine makes you feel uncomfortable, consider moderating coffee, energy drinks, and other caffeinated beverages.

Tap to view ›

Listen to Your Body

Notice how you feel and give yourself time to rest when your body needs extra care during your cycle.

Tap to view ›

Maintain a Regular Sleep Routine

Try to keep a consistent sleep and wake schedule to support adequate rest and recovery.

Tap to view ›

Know When to Seek Help

If menstrual pain or bleeding is severe, unusual, or interfering with daily activities, consider talking with a qualified healthcare professional.
